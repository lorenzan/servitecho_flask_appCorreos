Where do I get this data?
In your Google Ads account go to Tools → Conversions: you will find the Google Ads ID (format AW-XXXXXXXXX) and, when you create a conversion, the label that identifies it. The GA4 ID (format G-XXXXXXXXX, optional) is in Google Analytics 4 → Admin → Data streams.

While disabled, no Google scripts are loaded on the public site.

Used to record a conversion when a customer completes the quote form.

If filled in, Google Analytics 4 loads together with Google Ads (without duplicating the script).
