How do I get a Gmail app password?
Gmail does not use a traditional "API key" for sending email this way — it uses an app password. Steps: 1) Turn on 2-step verification on your Google account (myaccount.google.com/security). 2) Go to myaccount.google.com/apppasswords. 3) Create a new app password (choose "Other" and name it something like "Tecuns Roofing Web"). 4) Copy the 16-character code Google gives you and paste it below under "App password" — it is not your normal Gmail account password.
